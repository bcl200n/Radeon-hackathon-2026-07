Honest scope

What this does not claim

  • District boundaries are official; subdistrict and community tiers are constructed. The boundary file has no children, so the lower two tiers are an equal-population partition. Leader density is real; their affiliation is not.
  • Population is disaggregated at ~100 m (WorldPop R2025A, 3 arc-seconds ≈ 80 × 92 m at Chengdu's latitude), so block-level allocation interpolates below the source resolution.
  • Only Chengdu has an official shelter register. Comparison cities carry OpenStreetMap points with capacities from a fixed ladder, so cross-city comparison is restricted to dimensionless within-city quantities.
  • Congestion is under-represented — density uses whole-block area while people walk on streets.
  • Behavioural parameters are swept, not calibrated. No survey of shelter awareness exists for Chengdu.

Four earlier findings were published and then retracted; they are listed in claude/docs/ENGINEERING_LOG.md §8 and on the deck, with what each one turned out to be.
