Why the belief layer is the one worth watching

Only one thing in this whole city creates new information: somebody walks to a shelter and is turned away. That is public — a crowd held at the gate is visible — so at that instant every person in that block learns the shelter is full, and the block's believed wait for it jumps by 120 s. From then on, each step mixes every block's belief with its neighbours' and lets it decay.

Switch to the belief layer and the purple bloom is the rumour itself moving through the city, one block per step, reaching people who never went near that shelter and making them reroute before they waste the walk. Nobody has a god's-eye view: you know what you experienced, what a neighbour told you, and what a leader broadcast. That is the point — this is what distributed evacuation looks like when power, water and network are down and no central dispatcher exists.
